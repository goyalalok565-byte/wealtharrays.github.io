What an emergency fund target means

An emergency fund is a cash reserve intended for unexpected costs or income disruption. A common planning method is to express the target as a number of months of essential expenses rather than as a fixed amount.

Choosing the coverage period

Three, six or more months can make sense depending on job stability, household income, dependants, insurance coverage and access to other liquid assets. The calculator does not decide the right number for you; it makes your chosen assumption transparent.

What to include

Use essential recurring expenses such as housing, utilities, food, transport, insurance and minimum debt payments. Optional spending can be excluded when estimating the minimum reserve needed to keep essential bills covered.
