What this calculator does

Calculate a simple snapshot of assets, liabilities and estimated net worth. The result is generated locally from the values you choose, so the most useful way to use the tool is to test realistic assumptions instead of looking for a single “correct” future number.

Good financial planning is usually a range exercise. Try a conservative scenario, a middle scenario and a stressful scenario. The comparison feature is intended to make those trade-offs visible without opening separate calculators.

How the calculation works

Net worth is straightforward accounting: total assets minus total liabilities. The usefulness comes from defining inputs consistently and updating the snapshot over time.

The formula is deliberately transparent because a calculator should explain the logic behind the answer. A precise-looking number is not automatically a precise prediction: the mathematics can be correct while the assumptions are unrealistic. Keep those two ideas separate.

How to use the result responsibly

Start by entering values that reflect your actual situation. Avoid using a promotional interest rate, an unusually good market year or an optimistic income estimate as your only scenario. Change one assumption at a time and observe which input has the largest effect.

For decisions involving borrowing, investments or tax obligations, verify product-specific fees and rules separately. This tool is designed to make the arithmetic faster and easier to understand; it does not replace the terms of a bank, broker, employer, government authority or contract.

Worked scenario

Use the calculator above to build a baseline from your own numbers. Then open comparison mode and create a second scenario with a different rate, time period or amount. The difference between the two outputs is often more useful than either output alone because it shows how sensitive the decision is to your assumptions.

Limitations and methodology

Wealth Arrays uses deterministic formulas: identical inputs produce identical outputs. Real-world outcomes may differ because financial products can use different day-count conventions, compounding schedules, payment timing, fees, taxes, rounding rules and eligibility conditions. Where a country-specific rule matters, a generic calculator should not pretend to know it.

This page therefore presents the result as an estimate for education and planning. Check official documents and current rules before taking action based on a calculation.

Frequently asked questions

Is the net worth calculator result guaranteed?

No. This tool calculates the result implied by the assumptions you enter. Real financial outcomes can change because rates, fees, taxes, prices, contracts and timing can differ from a simplified model.

What assumptions matter most?

The most important inputs are the amounts, rates and time periods. Small changes can compound into large differences over longer horizons, so compare multiple scenarios rather than relying on one optimistic number.

Does this calculator include taxes and fees?

Only when an input explicitly asks for them. Otherwise treat the result as a pre-tax planning estimate and check the terms of your specific product or situation.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes. Wealth Arrays calculators run in the browser and are designed for touch input. Enter your own values and compare scenarios before making a decision.

Should I use this as financial advice?

No. The calculator is an educational planning tool. For regulated financial, tax or legal decisions, verify assumptions and consult an appropriately qualified professional when necessary.
