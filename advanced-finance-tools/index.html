EVERYDAY MONEY TOOLS

Make everyday money decisions with confidence.

Simple tools for tax, loans, rent, gold, budgeting and saving. No finance expertise needed.

How to use this page

1. Choose the question that matches your situation. 2. Enter your own numbers in the boxes. 3. Read the result shown underneath. Change a number to compare options.

Example: To compare two loan offers, enter the loan amount, years and both interest rates. The tool shows the estimated monthly payment and interest for each offer.
