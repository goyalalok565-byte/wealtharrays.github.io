How to contact Wealth Arrays

We welcome questions about calculators, corrections, broken links, unclear explanations and suggestions for future educational content.

For calculator corrections

Please include the calculator name, the exact values you entered, the result displayed and the result you expected. If possible, include the page URL and a short description of how the difference can be reproduced.

If an article contains an unclear statement, outdated information or a citation that needs review, tell us the specific section and what should be checked. For topics involving current tax rules, regulations, rates or financial products, include the relevant jurisdiction or product name.
