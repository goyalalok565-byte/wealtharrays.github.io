Verify important information

Interest rates, tax rules, fees, market conditions and financial product terms can change. Before making an important decision, verify current information with the relevant bank, lender, investment provider, employer, tax authority or other authoritative source.

Calculator limitations

Generic calculators cannot know every product-specific rule. They may not include every fee, tax, timing convention, rounding rule or eligibility condition. If a contract or official calculation differs from a generic estimate, the contract or applicable official rule should be used.

No personalised advice

Use of Wealth Arrays does not create an adviser-client, accountant-client, lawyer-client or lender-borrower relationship. Consider qualified professional advice when a decision depends on your individual circumstances or has significant financial consequences.
