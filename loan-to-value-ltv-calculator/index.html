What this calculator does

Calculate loan-to-value from a property value and loan amount. It keeps the assumptions visible so you can change one input at a time and understand what drives the result.

How the calculation works

LTV = loan amount ÷ property value × 100.

Inputs

The calculator uses property value and loan amount. Change the assumptions and click Calculate result to compare scenarios.

How to use the result responsibly

Do not treat one output as a prediction. Test conservative and optimistic assumptions, especially for rates, returns, inflation, taxes and property values. Compare total cost rather than judging an offer only by the monthly payment.

Limitations and methodology

Wealth Arrays performs deterministic browser-side calculations. It does not automatically know current market rates, lender policy, tax law, fees or personal eligibility. Verify current figures with the relevant official source or provider before making a material decision.

Frequently asked questions

Why does LTV matter?

It is one measure of collateral risk and can affect lending terms.

Does higher LTV mean higher EMI?

For the same rate and term, borrowing more produces a higher EMI.

Does property value always equal purchase price?

A lender may use its own valuation.
