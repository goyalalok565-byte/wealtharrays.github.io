Why Wealth Arrays shows scenarios

A single projected number can look more certain than its assumptions justify. Comparing conservative, base and higher-return cases makes sensitivity visible. Inflation-adjusted value is shown separately so nominal growth is not confused with purchasing power.

The target contribution is solved from the same deterministic model; it is not generated by AI.
