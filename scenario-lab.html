WEALTH ARRAYS 2.0

What if your money grows faster or slower?

Compare scenarios for your savings plan and see three possible outcomes. This helps you understand the impact of different assumptions — it cannot predict the future.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter your starting savings and monthly saving amount.
  2. Set your estimated yearly growth, inflation and number of years.
  3. Enter your future target if you have one.
  4. Press Compare my options. The results show lower, middle and higher scenarios side by side.

Tip: Change just one number at a time to see what makes the biggest difference.

Why Wealth Arrays shows scenarios

A single projected number can look more certain than its assumptions justify. Comparing conservative, base and higher-return cases makes sensitivity visible. Inflation-adjusted value is shown separately so nominal growth is not confused with purchasing power.

The target contribution is solved from the same deterministic model; it is not generated by AI.
