What this calculator does

Add a GST rate to a taxable amount to estimate the tax component and total invoice value. Change the assumptions to compare outcomes. The result is an estimate based on the inputs you provide, not a promise or personalised financial recommendation.

How the calculation works

GST amount = taxable amount × GST rate ÷ 100. Total invoice value = taxable amount + GST amount. This calculator adds tax to a pre-tax amount; it does not extract GST from a tax-inclusive price or decide which rate legally applies.

How to use this calculator

For this calculator, check these inputs and units: Taxable amount (before GST); GST rate; GST amount; Total including GST; Pre-tax amount. Keep monthly and annual figures separate, and change one input at a time when comparing scenarios.

Read the result in context

The output is the mathematical result of the assumptions entered, not a forecast of what a bank, employer, tax authority or investment provider will necessarily apply. Separate amounts you contribute or pay from interest, growth, tax or other calculated components where the tool provides them. A larger final amount is not always a better outcome: total interest, liquidity, affordability, risk and the time available can matter just as much as the headline number. Keep a note of your inputs when comparing two scenarios.

Compare more than one scenario

Run a baseline estimate first. Then create a conservative case and a second case with one important assumption changed, such as the rate, period, payment or contribution. Comparing scenarios is more informative than selecting a single result because it shows how sensitive the outcome is to the inputs. For long periods, even a small change in an assumed rate can have a large effect. For short periods, fees, timing and fixed charges can be relatively more important than a percentage-rate difference.

What this estimate does not include

This calculator does not automatically know current product terms, policy changes, eligibility conditions, fees, taxes, penalties, contribution ceilings or personal circumstances. Those items can change the real amount you receive or pay. For tax-related estimates, verify the applicable tax year, regime and official rules. For deposits, loans and retirement products, compare the result with the provider current documentation. If the result will affect a major financial decision, confirm the assumptions with a qualified professional or the relevant official source.

Example inputs

One illustrative set of inputs is: Taxable amount (before GST): 100000; GST rate: 18. Use your own figures for a more relevant estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Which GST rate should I use?

Enter the rate that applies to your specific goods or services. Rates and classifications depend on the transaction and current rules.

Does this split CGST and SGST?

No. It calculates the combined GST amount. Whether CGST/SGST or IGST applies depends on the transaction.

Important assumptions and limitations

This tool models the formula and assumptions described above. Actual outcomes can differ because of fees, taxes, eligibility rules, contribution timing, rate changes and individual circumstances. Check current official guidance or your provider's terms before making a financial decision.

GST arithmetic: exclusive price versus inclusive price

GST calculations depend on whether the entered price is before tax or already includes tax. For a pre-tax amount, GST = taxable value × rate ÷ 100 and invoice total = taxable value + GST. For a tax-inclusive total, the tax component is total × rate ÷ (100 + rate), and the taxable value is total ÷ (1 + rate/100). This calculator adds GST to a pre-tax amount; it does not extract tax from an inclusive total or decide the legally applicable rate.

Inclusive and exclusive prices are not interchangeable

Adding 18% to ₹1,00,000 gives ₹1,18,000. It is incorrect to take 18% of ₹1,18,000 to recover the tax already included; use the inclusive-price fraction instead. Invoice systems may separately show taxable value, CGST/SGST or IGST depending on the transaction. This calculator returns the combined tax amount and does not determine place-of-supply treatment or invoice compliance.

Verify the classification and current rules

GST rates depend on the classification of goods or services and applicable notifications. Whether CGST plus SGST or IGST applies depends on the transaction and place of supply. Inputting a rate does not mean that rate is legally correct. Verify the current GST portal guidance or consult a tax professional for invoice, registration and filing questions.

Worked example with Indian rupees

For a pre-tax taxable value of ₹1,00,000 at an illustrative 18% rate, GST is ₹18,000 and the invoice total is ₹1,18,000. If ₹1,18,000 is already GST-inclusive at 18%, the tax component is ₹18,000 and the pre-tax value is ₹1,00,000. Those results are equivalent only because the example uses a matching rate and amount. Actual rates and classifications must be verified for the goods or services.
