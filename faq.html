Frequently asked questions

Answers about Wealth Arrays calculators, formulas, assumptions, currency display, privacy, content and responsible use.

Using the calculators

Are Wealth Arrays calculators free?

Yes. The core calculator library is free to use and does not require an account. Each tool is designed to perform its calculation in the browser from the values you enter.

How are calculations performed?

Each calculator uses a defined mathematical model. Individual calculator pages explain the main formula, the meaning of important inputs and the limitations of the result. The calculations are performed locally in the browser rather than by connecting to your bank or investment account.

Why does the site show an estimate instead of a prediction?

Inputs such as interest rates, investment returns, inflation and future working hours can be uncertain. The calculator can compute the selected assumptions precisely, but it cannot know the future or the terms of every financial product. Treat the output as a scenario, not a guarantee.

Should I compare more than one scenario?

Yes. For long-term financial planning, changing one assumption at a time can show how sensitive the result is. Comparing conservative, middle and more optimistic cases is generally more informative than relying on a single precise-looking number.

Currency and accuracy

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Currency selection changes the display unit and formatting for supported monetary results. It does not automatically perform live foreign-exchange conversion unless a specific calculator explicitly says it does.

Why might my real result differ from the calculator?

Real products can have fees, taxes, payment timing, rounding, variable rates, eligibility rules and other conditions. Investment returns can also vary over time. A generic calculator cannot automatically reproduce every contract or jurisdiction-specific rule.

How do I report a calculation problem?

Send the calculator name, the exact inputs you entered, the result you received and what you expected to goyalalok565@gmail.com. Reproducible examples make technical and formula corrections much easier.

Financial and tax use

Are the calculators financial advice?

No. They are educational planning tools. They do not know your complete financial situation and should not be treated as personalised investment, tax or legal advice.

Can I use the tax calculator to file a return?

No. Tax rules depend on jurisdiction, filing status, tax year, deductions, credits and income types. Use official tax authority guidance or approved filing software for an actual return.

Can I use the loan calculator to decide whether a loan is affordable?

It can help you understand payment mathematics, but affordability also depends on income, expenses, fees, insurance, rate changes and the lender's contract. Review the complete loan terms before making a borrowing decision.

Privacy and content

Do you copy articles from other websites?

We publish original educational material for Wealth Arrays. Guides are written to explain formulas, examples, assumptions and limitations rather than simply reproduce information from another site.

Does Wealth Arrays need my bank or investment login?

No. The calculators do not require a bank or brokerage login to perform their basic calculations. Enter the scenario values directly into the tool.

Can I contact Wealth Arrays?
