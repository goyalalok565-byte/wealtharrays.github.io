Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about our calculators, formulas, currencies, privacy, content and how to use the site.

Using the calculators

Are Wealth Arrays calculators free?

Yes. The core financial calculators are free to use and do not require an account.

Can I use the calculators on my phone?

Yes. The interface is mobile-first, responsive and designed for touch screens without intentional horizontal scrolling.

How are calculations performed?

The calculators use standard mathematical and financial formulas implemented in client-side JavaScript. Each calculator includes an explanation of its main formula and assumptions.

Currency & accuracy

Can I change the currency?

Yes. More than 100 currency choices are available for displaying monetary results. Currency selection changes the display unit; it does not perform live foreign-exchange conversion.

Are calculator results financial advice?

No. Results are educational estimates based on the assumptions you enter. Taxes, fees, inflation, product rules and personal circumstances can change real outcomes.

Privacy & content

Do you copy articles from other websites?

We publish original educational material written for Wealth Arrays. We avoid copying or republishing third-party articles and focus on useful explanations, examples, formulas and limitations.
