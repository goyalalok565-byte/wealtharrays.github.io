ABOUT WEALTH ARRAYS

Built to make financial calculations inspectable.

Wealth Arrays is a financial education and calculation platform created by Alok Goyal. The site combines browser-based calculators with original guides so readers can inspect the arithmetic, change assumptions and understand limitations instead of relying on an unexplained headline number.

What we publish

We publish calculators for investing, borrowing, interest, returns, retirement planning, income and small-business calculations, alongside original educational guides. The calculator is the interactive layer; the guides and methodology explain what the number means and what it leaves out.

Who creates and maintains the site

Alok Goyal is the founder and creator of Wealth Arrays and is responsible for the site's product direction, calculator implementation and published educational framework. We do not claim professional financial, tax, legal or investment credentials that are not stated here.

How calculator work is developed

Calculator definitions, page content and runtime code are maintained in the source repository and deployed through a repeatable build pipeline. Important changes are checked against existing calculator and site behaviour. This source-first approach means a correction is applied to the underlying implementation so a later deployment does not silently restore an old bug.

How we test

Testing focuses on formula cases, boundary conditions, displayed results, links, canonical routes and important interactive behaviour. For financial models, we separately consider whether arithmetic is correct and whether assumptions are appropriate to the question. A generic calculator cannot reproduce every lender, bank, tax authority or investment provider's exact rules.

How we handle corrections

If a calculation, explanation or link is wrong, readers can report it with the page URL, exact inputs and expected result or source of the discrepancy. Confirmed problems are corrected in the underlying source and rebuilt rather than hidden with a one-off page edit.

Editorial approach

We prefer original explanations, worked examples, explicit assumptions and practical limitations. We do not promise outcomes or change a number simply because it looks more persuasive. When a claim depends on current law, tax rules, rates or product terms, readers are directed to the relevant authoritative source.

What Wealth Arrays does not claim

The site does not predict market returns, guarantee loan approval, determine an individual's tax liability or replace personalised professional advice.
