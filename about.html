ABOUT WEALTH ARRAYS

Useful numbers, clearly explained.

Wealth Arrays is a free collection of browser-first financial calculators and original educational guides. The site is maintained by Wealth Arrays and is designed around transparent formulas, stated assumptions and practical explanations.

Who maintains the site

Wealth Arrays is the publisher and editorial owner of this website. The publisher is responsible for calculator definitions, published guides, corrections and editorial standards. We do not present ourselves as a regulated financial adviser, tax professional or lender.

How calculators are reviewed

Calculator definitions use explicit formulas and named input fields. Deterministic test cases are run against the calculator library as part of release validation. Results are checked for finite outputs and basic input-definition integrity. If you find an error, send the exact inputs and result so it can be reproduced and reviewed.

How our guides are written

Our guides are written for Wealth Arrays rather than copied from third-party publishers. We explain relevant mathematics, show practical reasoning, identify assumptions and state important limitations. A guide may discuss a financial concept without recommending a particular product.

Editorial independence

Advertising, if enabled, does not buy editorial placement or change calculator mathematics. We do not promise returns, approval, savings or financial outcomes. Content is educational and should not replace professional financial, tax, legal or investment advice.

Corrections and review

Content and calculator logic can be corrected when an error is identified. Material updates are reviewed against the relevant formula or assumption and the page review date is updated when appropriate.

Privacy by design

Core calculator mathematics is designed to run in the browser. Do not enter passwords, payment-card information or other sensitive information into a public website. See the Privacy Policy for analytics and advertising information.
