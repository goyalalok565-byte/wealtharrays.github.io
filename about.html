ABOUT WEALTH ARRAYS

Useful numbers, clearly explained.

Wealth Arrays is a free website with financial calculators and practical guides. The goal is simple: help people understand common money calculations without making the math harder than it needs to be.

Our calculators use clear formulas, named inputs and stated assumptions. We test the calculator code before releases and review reported errors. Our guides are written for this site and explain the math, examples and limits in plain English. They are educational and are not personal financial, tax, legal or investment advice.

Advertising, if enabled, does not change calculator results or editorial content. If you spot a mistake or have a useful correction, send the details and we will review them.

How calculators are checked

We run repeatable test cases against the calculator library and check that results are valid and the inputs match the stated calculation. When an issue is reported, we try to reproduce it before making a change.

How guides are written

Guides are written for Wealth Arrays rather than copied from other sites. We use examples and simple explanations to show how a calculation works and where the result may not apply.

Privacy

Core calculator math runs in your browser. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information into a public website. See the Privacy Policy for analytics and advertising information.
