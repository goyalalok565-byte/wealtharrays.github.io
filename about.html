About Wealth Arrays

What Wealth Arrays publishes

Wealth Arrays publishes browser-based financial calculators and original educational guides. The calculators cover topics such as investing, borrowing, interest, returns, income and planning. Each tool is intended to make a defined calculation easier to understand, while the accompanying explanations provide context about assumptions and limitations.

How we approach calculator content

We document the main formula or calculation method, explain important inputs and provide examples where they help interpretation. We distinguish an arithmetic result from a forecast: a calculator can compute the result implied by an assumption, but it cannot guarantee that the assumption will occur in real life.

Editorial approach

Our content is written for clarity and practical usefulness. We prefer plain-language explanations, reproducible examples and explicit limitations over unexplained numbers. When a subject depends on current laws, tax rules, rates or product terms, readers should verify the current details with the relevant authoritative source.

Corrections and feedback

If you find a calculation error, broken link or unclear explanation, please send the page URL, the inputs involved and a description of the problem to goyalalok565@gmail.com. Reproducible examples help us investigate and correct issues at the source.

Who the site is for

Wealth Arrays is intended for people who want to understand the mathematics behind common money questions before making a decision. That includes someone comparing a monthly investment amount, checking how a loan payment changes with tenure, understanding inflation, or exploring how a return is annualized. The site is designed to make the calculation itself transparent rather than asking readers to accept an unexplained number.

How pages are maintained

Calculator logic and page content are maintained in the site's source repository and deployed through a repeatable build process. This matters because a correction should survive the next deployment instead of being a one-page manual edit. We also use regression checks for important calculator and site functionality when source changes are made.

What we do not claim

We do not claim to predict market returns, guarantee a loan outcome, determine a person's tax liability, or replace professional advice. Where a calculation depends on current rules or a specific financial contract, we point readers back to the relevant official or product-specific information. The purpose of Wealth Arrays is to improve understanding of the underlying calculation.
