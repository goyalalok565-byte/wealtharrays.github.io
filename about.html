About Wealth Arrays

Wealth Arrays is a free collection of financial calculators — investment, loan, banking, retirement, salary, and business tools — built to give clear, instant answers without any catch.

Why we built this

Most financial calculator sites make money by selling your contact details to advisors, or by nudging you toward a specific product. We don't do either. Wealth Arrays runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere, and there's no sign-up wall between you and a result.

How it's funded

The site is supported by advertising, shown alongside the tools — not instead of them. We don't sell user data, and we don't accept payment to recommend a particular loan, fund, or advisor.

Accuracy

Every calculator uses standard, published financial formulas (the same ones used in banking and finance textbooks). Results are estimates for planning purposes — they don't account for taxes, fees, or your personal circumstances, and shouldn't replace advice from a qualified professional.

Wealth Arrays gives free, unbiased calculators — no advisor referrals, no data selling. Results are estimates for planning purposes, not financial advice.
