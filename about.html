ABOUT WEALTH ARRAYS

Useful numbers, clearly explained.

Wealth Arrays is a free collection of financial calculators and original educational guides for everyday money decisions.

What we build

We create calculators for investing, loans, banking, retirement, salary and business questions. The goal is not to sell a financial product; it is to make the underlying math easier to understand.

How we approach accuracy

Each calculator uses a defined formula and exposes the important assumptions. Results are estimates. Taxes, fees, inflation, market volatility, lender rules and personal circumstances can change real outcomes.

Our content policy

Our guides are written specifically for Wealth Arrays. We do not scrape or republish other publishers' articles. We add our own explanations, examples, formulas and limitations rather than producing pages that simply repeat search results.

Privacy

Calculators are designed to perform their core mathematics in your browser. Never enter passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information into a public website.
