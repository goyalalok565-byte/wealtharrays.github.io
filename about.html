ABOUT WEALTH ARRAYS

Financial tools built to make numbers clearer.

Wealth Arrays is a free platform for financial calculators and practical guides. We build tools that help investors, savers and everyday planners understand common financial calculations without unnecessary complexity.

Meet the founder

Alok Goyal is the Founder & Creator of Wealth Arrays, based in New Delhi, India. As an active market investor, Alok frequently used online financial calculators while evaluating ideas and planning investments.

Over time, he found that many existing tools were slow, crowded with ads, difficult to use or light on clear explanations. That experience led him to build Wealth Arrays: a cleaner place where people around the world can run financial calculations, understand the assumptions behind them and use the results without unnecessary friction.

Our mission

The mission of Wealth Arrays is straightforward: make useful financial calculations fast, transparent and easy to understand for a global audience.

  • Ultra-fast and focused: keep the experience lightweight, responsive and free from unnecessary clutter.
  • Transparent calculations: show clear inputs, formulas, assumptions and explanations so users can understand how a result is produced.
  • Premium user experience: build smooth, accessible tools that work well across modern devices and for people in different markets.

How we build and check our tools

Our calculators use explicit formulas, named inputs and stated assumptions. We run repeatable test cases against the calculator library and check that results remain valid as the code evolves. When a problem is reported, we try to reproduce it before making a change.

Our guides are written specifically for Wealth Arrays. They use practical examples and plain English to explain calculations, assumptions and limitations. Calculator results are educational estimates and should not be treated as personal financial, tax, legal or investment advice.

Clear information, no hidden agenda

Advertising, when enabled, does not change calculator formulas or editorial content. We aim to explain both what a calculation can tell you and where its limits are. If you find an error, an unclear explanation or something that should be updated, we welcome corrections.
