ADVERTISING & MONETIZATION

How Wealth Arrays keeps monetization from getting in the way.

We build calculators to help users understand financial numbers. Revenue must not make a calculator harder to use, change a mathematical result or disguise advertising as independent guidance.

Current approach

Advertising may be introduced only after the site meets the applicable requirements of the advertising partner and the implementation can preserve a clear user experience. We do not place fake ads or misleading ad placeholders.

Calculator-first placement rules

If ads are introduced, they should not be placed inside numeric input flows, immediately beside the primary Calculate button, or in a way that encourages accidental clicks. A user must be able to enter values and understand a result without advertising interrupting the calculation.

Clear separation

Paid advertising, sponsorships and affiliate relationships must be distinguishable from independent educational content. Commercial arrangements do not determine calculator formulas or calculated outputs.

No paid manipulation of results

No advertiser or partner may pay Wealth Arrays to alter a formula, inflate a projected outcome or change an educational conclusion to favor a product.

Privacy

Any future advertising technology may use cookies or similar technologies according to applicable partner policies and legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected in our Privacy Policy.

Corrections and independence

Our Editorial Standards apply independently of advertising. Report concerns through Contact.
