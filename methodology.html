METHODOLOGY

How our calculators work.

Wealth Arrays provides educational financial calculators. Calculations are performed in your browser from the values you enter and the formulas described for each tool.

Formulas

Each calculator is built around a defined mathematical model. Investment tools use future-value and compounding formulas; loan tools use standard amortization mathematics; percentage and business tools use their respective arithmetic definitions. Calculator pages explain the main formula and assumptions relevant to that tool.

Assumptions and estimates

Future returns, inflation, tax rates and similar inputs are assumptions unless a calculator explicitly states otherwise. Changing an assumption can materially change a result. A projection is not a prediction or guarantee.

Currency display

Changing the site currency changes the display format used for monetary results. It does not perform live foreign-exchange conversion unless a tool explicitly says it does.

Accuracy review

We test formulas against known scenarios and review reported issues. If you find a problem, send the calculator name, exact inputs and the result you expected to goyalalok565@gmail.com.

Not financial, tax or legal advice

These tools are educational estimates. Your actual financial outcome may differ because of fees, taxes, timing, regulations, eligibility rules and other factors. Consult an appropriately qualified professional for decisions requiring personal advice.
