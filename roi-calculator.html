How it's calculated

Total ROI = (Final value − Cost) / Cost × 100. Annualized ROI = [(Final value / Cost)^(1/years) − 1] × 100 — this spreads the total return evenly across each year, so investments held for different lengths of time can be compared fairly.

Worked example

With amount invested of 10000, current / final value of 14500, holding period of 3yrs, this works out to a net gain of $4,500.00, total roi of 45.00%, annualized roi of 13.19%. Try it yourself in the calculator above with your own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between ROI and CAGR?

Annualized ROI here is calculated the same way as CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) — both express a multi-year return as an equivalent constant yearly rate.

Does ROI include fees or taxes?

Only if you build them into your cost or final value inputs. The formula itself is agnostic — it just compares two numbers.

What does a negative ROI mean?

It means the final value was lower than what you put in — you lost money on the investment or purchase over that period.
