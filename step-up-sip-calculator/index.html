What this calculator does

Estimate how an annual increase in monthly SIP contributions may change the projected corpus. Change the assumptions to compare outcomes. The result is an estimate based on the inputs you provide, not a promise or personalised financial recommendation.

How the calculation works

The monthly contribution increases by the step-up percentage at the start of each investment year. Each contribution is added at the start of a month and the balance grows at the monthly equivalent of the assumed annual return. Actual investment returns vary and are not guaranteed.

How to use this calculator

Start by entering values that match your situation and check the unit shown beside each input. For a useful first estimate, use a realistic starting point rather than an ideal outcome. Review every result row, then change one input at a time so you can see which assumption has the largest effect. If a field represents an annual rate, keep it annual unless the label explicitly says monthly. If a field represents a contribution or payment, confirm whether it is monthly or annual before interpreting the total.

Read the result in context

The output is the mathematical result of the assumptions entered, not a forecast of what a bank, employer, tax authority or investment provider will necessarily apply. Separate amounts you contribute or pay from interest, growth, tax or other calculated components where the tool provides them. A larger final amount is not always a better outcome: total interest, liquidity, affordability, risk and the time available can matter just as much as the headline number. Keep a note of your inputs when comparing two scenarios.

Compare more than one scenario

Run a baseline estimate first. Then create a conservative case and a second case with one important assumption changed, such as the rate, period, payment or contribution. Comparing scenarios is more informative than selecting a single result because it shows how sensitive the outcome is to the inputs. For long periods, even a small change in an assumed rate can have a large effect. For short periods, fees, timing and fixed charges can be relatively more important than a percentage-rate difference.

What this estimate does not include

This calculator does not automatically know current product terms, policy changes, eligibility conditions, fees, taxes, penalties, contribution ceilings or personal circumstances. Those items can change the real amount you receive or pay. For tax-related estimates, verify the applicable tax year, regime and official rules. For deposits, loans and retirement products, compare the result with the provider current documentation. If the result will affect a major financial decision, confirm the assumptions with a qualified professional or the relevant official source.

Example inputs

One illustrative set of inputs is: Starting monthly investment: 5000; Annual SIP increase: 10; Assumed annual return: 12; Investment period: 15. Use your own figures for a more relevant estimate.

Frequently asked questions

What is a step-up SIP?

It is a recurring investment plan where the monthly contribution increases periodically, often once a year, as income or savings capacity grows.

Are the projected returns guaranteed?

No. The return rate is an assumption. Market-linked investments can lose value and actual returns vary over time.

Important assumptions and limitations

This tool models the formula and assumptions described above. Actual outcomes can differ because of fees, taxes, eligibility rules, contribution timing, rate changes and individual circumstances. Check current official guidance or your provider's terms before making a financial decision.
