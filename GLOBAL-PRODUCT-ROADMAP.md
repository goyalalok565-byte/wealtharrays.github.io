# Wealth Arrays Global Product Roadmap

North-star: become the place where people model how financial decisions change their future.

Product ladder: calculators -> scenario engine -> goal engine -> decision workspace -> AI interface -> global platform/API.

Permanent principles: reproducible calculations; visible assumptions; scenario comparison; transparent methodology; AI explains but does not invent numbers; privacy by design; country-specific rules separated from global mathematics; no dark patterns; no thin programmatic SEO; accessibility/mobile/regression QA as release gates.

Current release: Scenario Lab foundation, deterministic financial engine, scenario comparison, sensitivity analysis and goal solver.

Next releases: equivalence-tested adapters for SIP/compound/inflation/retirement; one-click scenario comparison; goal workflows; shareable reproducible scenarios; retirement stress testing; documented Monte Carlo only after statistical QA; country modules; multilingual UI/content; saved workspace; auditable reports; AI intent layer; API.

Do not ship yet: generic chatbot, black-box financial predictions, unverified tax automation, account linking, mass SEO pages, or changes to stable calculator/PDF/theme/currency behavior without regression proof.
