# Wealth Arrays Global Product Roadmap

North-star: become the place where people model how financial decisions change their future.

Product ladder: calculators -> scenario engine -> goal engine -> decision workspace -> AI interface -> global platform/API.

Permanent principles: reproducible calculations; visible assumptions; scenario comparison; transparent methodology; AI explains but does not invent numbers; privacy by design; country-specific rules separated from global mathematics; no dark patterns; no thin programmatic SEO; accessibility/mobile/regression QA as release gates.

Current release: Scenario Lab + Goal Planner foundation, deterministic financial engine, scenario comparison, sensitivity analysis, goal solver and certified non-invasive SIP, compound-interest, inflation and retirement adapters. Adapter outputs are equivalence-tested against the legacy calculator formulas before they can be used by future decision workflows.

Next releases: equivalence-tested adapters for additional calculator families; one-click scenario comparison; shareable reproducible scenarios; deterministic stress testing; retirement stress testing; documented Monte Carlo only after statistical QA; country modules with source/effective-date provenance; multilingual UI/content with local financial context; saved workspace; auditable reports; AI intent layer; API.

Global leadership workstreams:
1. Calculation integrity — formula registry, golden tests, edge-case tests, timing conventions and cross-calculator equivalence.
2. Decision intelligence — scenario comparison, stress testing, goal solving, trade-off views and eventually probability models with statistical QA.
3. Trust — methodology, authorship, corrections, source provenance, model/version identifiers and reproducible reports.
4. Globalization — country modules separated from universal math, jurisdiction/year-aware rules, local terminology and currencies.
5. Accessibility and UX — keyboard/screen-reader support, mobile performance, clear input validation and understandable explanations.
6. Distribution — non-brand search coverage, topical authority, internal linking, structured data, machine-readable documentation and genuine editorial originality.
7. Privacy — browser-first calculations, minimal data collection, explicit consent, export/delete controls before any saved-account workflow.
8. AI interface — intent parsing and explanation layered on top of deterministic engines; no invented financial numbers, black-box recommendations or unsupported tax claims.
9. Platform — saved scenarios, reproducible reports, share links, API contracts and eventually partner/developer access.
10. Business durability — useful free core, transparent monetization, no ad UX that interferes with calculations, and measurable product analytics that do not compromise privacy.

Do not ship yet: generic chatbot, black-box financial predictions, unverified tax automation, account linking, mass SEO pages, or changes to stable calculator/PDF/theme/currency behavior without regression proof.
