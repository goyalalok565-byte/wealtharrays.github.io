EDITORIAL STANDARDS

How we create, review and correct our content.

Editor and publisher: Wealth Arrays, created by Alok Goyal.

Wealth Arrays publishes calculators and educational explanations to help people understand financial mathematics. We aim for clarity, originality, transparent assumptions and reproducible examples rather than promises about personalised outcomes.

1. Originality and purpose

Pages are created for a specific user question or calculator. We do not publish scraped copies of other publishers' articles as original work. Adding words without adding useful information is not our content standard.

2. Calculator review

Each tool is based on a defined mathematical model. We document formulas and material conventions such as compounding, contribution timing, loan-rate assumptions and rounding. The methodology reference provides the central model documentation.

3. Evidence and current information

For claims that depend on current tax rules, laws, rates, regulations or product terms, the relevant official or product-specific source takes precedence. Historical or research-based concepts are described with their limitations rather than presented as guarantees. We do not invent sources, statistics, credentials, reviews or experience.

4. Accuracy and testing

We test representative calculations, boundary conditions and important site behaviour. A useful error report includes the page, exact inputs, displayed output and expected output or source.

5. Corrections

Confirmed errors are corrected in source implementation or source content and then rebuilt. We do not alter formulas to produce a preferred-looking result.

6. Authorship and responsibility

Wealth Arrays identifies Alok Goyal as founder and creator. Article pages identify their maintenance responsibility, while the About and Methodology pages explain the scope and limitations of the site's work. We do not imply professional credentials that have not been stated.

7. Advertising and editorial independence

Advertising should not determine calculator mathematics, editorial conclusions or correction decisions. Sponsored or promotional material, if introduced, should be distinguishable from independent educational content.

8. Financial and legal limitations

Wealth Arrays is not a government tax service, lender underwriting system, investment manager or substitute for personalised professional advice. Generic calculations cannot know every fee, tax, eligibility rule, contract term or market outcome.
