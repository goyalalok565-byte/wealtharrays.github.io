EDITORIAL STANDARDS

How we create and correct our content.

Wealth Arrays publishes calculators and educational explanations to help people understand financial mathematics. We aim for clarity, originality and transparent assumptions rather than promising personalized financial outcomes.

Originality

Our pages are created for Wealth Arrays. We do not publish scraped copies of other publishers' articles as original work. Explanations are written around the calculator, its formula, assumptions, examples and practical limitations.

Calculator methodology

Each tool is based on a defined mathematical model. Where a result depends on assumptions such as a future return, interest rate, inflation rate or tax treatment, those assumptions can materially change the output. Projections are estimates, not guarantees.

Accuracy review

We review calculator logic against known scenarios and investigate credible reports of errors. A useful correction report includes the calculator name, exact inputs, the result shown and the expected result or source of the concern.

Corrections and updates

If we confirm an error, we correct the affected calculation or explanation. Material updates should improve accuracy or clarity; we do not change formulas merely to produce a more attractive financial result.

Editorial independence

Advertising or commercial relationships must not determine a calculator's mathematical result or the factual conclusion of an educational explanation. Sponsored or promotional material, if introduced in the future, should be distinguishable from independent educational content.

Not personal advice

Wealth Arrays does not know your complete financial situation. Our calculators and guides are general educational tools and are not substitutes for personalized financial, tax, legal, accounting or investment advice.
