How it's calculated

Hourly rate = Annual salary ÷ (Hours per week × Working weeks per year). To go the other way: Annual salary = Hourly rate × Hours per week × Working weeks per year.

Worked example

With convert of salary → hourly, amount of 60000, hours per week of 40, working weeks per year of 48, this works out to a working hours / year of 1,920, hourly rate of $31.25. Try it yourself in the calculator above with your own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Why 48 weeks and not 52?

52 weeks minus a typical 2–4 weeks of unpaid time off (holidays, sick days not covered by salary) gives a more realistic working-weeks figure. Adjust it to match your actual situation — salaried roles with full paid leave might use 52.

Does this include overtime?

No — this is a straight-line conversion based on regular hours. If you regularly work overtime, your effective hourly rate for salaried work will be lower than shown here.

Are taxes included?

No, both figures are gross (pre-tax) amounts.
