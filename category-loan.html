HOW TO USE THIS COLLECTION

Understand the calculation before using the result

Loan calculators translate principal, interest rate and repayment period into an estimated payment schedule. They are useful when comparing scenarios because a lower monthly payment can come with a longer repayment period and higher total interest.

Before using a result for an actual loan, check the lender's current offer. Look for the annual interest rate, whether the rate is fixed or variable, processing fees, insurance, prepayment rules and the exact payment frequency. These details can make the contractual cost different from a generic calculation.

For comparison, run a baseline scenario and then change one variable such as tenure or rate. Looking at both the payment and total interest gives more context than looking at the monthly amount alone. The result is an estimate, not a loan approval or affordability assessment.
