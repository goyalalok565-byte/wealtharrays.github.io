Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Overview

Wealth Arrays provides browser-first financial calculators. Many calculations run locally in your browser. Some optional features—such as account sign-in, cloud scenario sync, live exchange rates, tax estimates, analytics and advertising—make network requests as described below. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, bank account numbers or other sensitive information into calculator fields or shared links.

Information processed by optional features

If you create an account, our configured authentication provider (Supabase, when enabled) processes your email address, authentication credentials and session data to authenticate you. Wealth Arrays does not store your raw password in its own database. If you explicitly sync a scenario to your account, the selected calculator identifier, scenario name, inputs and any submitted results are stored in the site's database so you can retrieve or delete them. Account and cloud-sync features may be unavailable until the service is configured.

If you use live currency conversion or a server-backed tax estimate, the requested currency pair or tax inputs are sent to the relevant endpoint to provide the requested result. Avoid including identifying or sensitive personal information in these requests.

Browser storage and shared links

The site may store preferences such as currency and theme, locally saved scenarios, consent choices, and an authentication session in your browser's local storage. Local browser storage remains on that browser unless you clear it or the application removes it; it is not automatically synchronized to an account. Signing out removes the locally stored authentication session. A scenario-sharing link may include the calculator inputs in its URL. Anyone with that link may be able to read those values, and the URL may appear in browser history or systems that handle links. Share links only when you are comfortable disclosing their contents.

Subscriptions and payments

If you choose a paid subscription, Razorpay processes checkout and subscription payments under its own terms and privacy policy. Wealth Arrays stores subscription identifiers, plan/status information and period dates to operate the subscription. Signed provider webhook events are recorded to reconcile subscription status. Do not send card details directly to Wealth Arrays. Subscription availability and entitlements depend on the server-side configuration of the deployed service.

Analytics and advertising

When enabled and permitted by your choices, Google Analytics may process usage information to help us understand aggregate traffic and improve the site. We do not intentionally send calculator input values as analytics events. Google AdSense and related advertising technology may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, personalize or measure ads according to the provider's policies and applicable consent requirements. You can manage Google advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings and learn about industry opt-outs at YourAdChoices. Where required, advertising consent tools are used before personalized advertising is served. Where enabled, Google Privacy & Messaging / Funding Choices manages advertising consent messages; its message script and configuration are provided by Google, and consent choices may be stored by the provider or in your browser.

Service providers, retention and security

Depending on which features you use and how the deployment is configured, service providers may include Cloudflare (hosting and API delivery), Supabase (authentication), Razorpay (payments), and Google (analytics and advertising). We retain account-linked scenarios while they remain in your account or until deletion is requested and completed, subject to backups and operational constraints. Billing and security records may be retained where reasonably necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; keep your account credentials private and sign out on shared devices.

Your choices and requests

You can clear local site data through your browser, delete locally saved scenarios in the calculator, and delete cloud scenarios from the account page when those features are available. For requests to access, correct or delete account-linked information, contact goyalalok565@gmail.com from the email associated with the account. We may need to verify your request and retain limited records where legally required. Analytics and advertising consent choices are separate and may be revisited through the available consent controls.

Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International use

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Privacy rights and requirements vary by jurisdiction. We will handle requests under applicable law. This policy is operational product information, not legal advice, and should be reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions in which the service operates.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product or applicable requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
