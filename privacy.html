Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

What we don't collect

Wealth Arrays has no sign-up or login. The numbers you enter into a calculator are processed entirely in your own browser and are never sent to our servers — we don't see them, store them, or have any way to connect them to you.

What is stored locally

Your chosen currency and light/dark theme preference are saved in your browser's local storage, purely so the site remembers your choice on your next visit. This stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Advertising cookies

If advertising is enabled on Wealth Arrays in the future, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. Any advertising implementation will be reflected in this policy when it goes live. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings, and generally through www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use standard analytics tools to understand overall traffic patterns (such as which pages are visited and roughly how often), in aggregate — not tied to your identity.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date.
