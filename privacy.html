Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

What we don't collect

Wealth Arrays has no sign-up or login. Core calculator inputs are designed to be processed in your browser and are not intentionally sent to Wealth Arrays servers. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information into a public website.

What is stored locally

Your chosen currency and light/dark theme preference are saved in your browser's local storage so the site can remember your choices. This local storage is not a server-side account or profile.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns and improve the site. Analytics is optional through the privacy choices shown on the site. When analytics is denied, the site's analytics consent state remains denied. We do not intentionally send calculator input values as analytics events.

Advertising

At the time of this update, Wealth Arrays does not serve Google AdSense advertisements. If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising technology may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve and measure ads according to the applicable provider policies and user consent requirements.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google may use cookies to personalize or measure advertising where permitted. Users can manage Google's advertising choices through Google Ads Settings. Information about opting out of interest-based advertising is also available through YourAdChoices.

Where required for applicable regions, advertising consent will be handled through a Google-supported or Google-certified consent solution before personalized advertising is served.

Privacy choices

The site may show a privacy notice asking whether you permit analytics. You can change or reset this choice by clearing the site's browser data. Advertising consent, when advertising is live, will be presented separately where required.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date.
