How to use the savings goal planner

  1. Enter the total amount you want to reach.
  2. Add how much you have already saved and how much you save each month.
  3. Choose the number of years and your estimated growth and inflation rates.
  4. Press Calculate my goal to see whether your current plan may reach the target.

Tip: If there is a gap, try saving more each month or allowing more time. Investment returns are not guaranteed.

What this does differently

A target by itself is not a plan. Wealth Arrays connects the target to time, starting capital, contribution rate, contribution growth, return and inflation assumptions. The same deterministic model is then used to show how sensitive the goal is to those assumptions.

The result is a mathematical scenario, not a promise or personalised financial advice. Taxes, fees, irregular cash flows and product-specific rules may require a different model.
