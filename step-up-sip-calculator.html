How a Step-Up SIP works

A Step-Up SIP starts with a monthly contribution and increases that contribution at a regular annual rate. This calculator applies the increase every 12 months and compounds each monthly contribution at the expected return.

Why the step-up matters

If income rises over time, keeping an investment contribution fixed can leave savings growth behind. A planned annual increase lets contributions grow gradually instead of requiring a large jump at the start.

Important assumptions

The return is an assumption, not a guaranteed result. The calculation does not model fund fees, taxes, changing market returns or irregular contributions. Inflation is shown separately so you can compare the projected future value with today's purchasing power.
