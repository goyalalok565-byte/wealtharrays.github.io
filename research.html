ORIGINAL RESEARCH

Wealth Arrays Research

Reproducible financial calculations built from explicit assumptions—not forecasts, rankings or hidden models.

We publish small, testable studies that show exactly how a financial assumption changes a mathematical result. Each study states its inputs, formula convention, scenario range and limitations so another reader can reproduce the calculation.

SIP contribution timing

Compares recurring contributions made at different points in the compounding period and shows why contribution timing changes the mathematical outcome even when the monthly amount, rate and horizon are unchanged.

Method: fixed contribution, fixed annual assumption, fixed horizon, deterministic monthly compounding.

Read the SIP scenario methodology →

EMI versus total borrowing cost

Shows why a lower monthly payment can coexist with a higher total interest cost when the repayment term changes. The study keeps principal and rate explicit so the trade-off is visible.

Method: standard amortization equation with identical principal/rate assumptions across scenarios.

Read the EMI cost analysis →

Nominal return versus real purchasing power

Demonstrates the difference between a future nominal balance and its inflation-adjusted purchasing power. The purpose is to separate arithmetic growth from what the amount may buy later.

Method: nominal future value compared with inflation-adjusted value under explicit constant-rate scenarios.

Research standards

  • Inputs and assumptions are visible.
  • Calculations are deterministic and reproducible.
  • Scenario outputs are not presented as guaranteed investment outcomes.
  • Current regulatory or economic facts are linked to primary sources where relevant.
  • Limitations are stated instead of hidden.
