Profit Margin

Calculate gross margin (revenue minus cost of goods) and net margin (after all expenses) as percentages of revenue.

Tool info
CategoryBusiness Calculators

How it's calculated

Gross margin = (Revenue − Cost of goods sold) ÷ Revenue × 100. Net margin = (Revenue − Cost of goods sold − Other expenses) ÷ Revenue × 100.

Worked example

With revenue of 50000, cost of goods sold of 28000, other operating expenses of 9000, this works out to a gross profit of $22,000.00, gross margin of 44.00%, net profit of $13,000.00, net margin of 26.00%. Try it yourself in the calculator above with your own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between gross and net margin?

Gross margin only subtracts the direct cost of producing what you sold. Net margin also subtracts everything else — rent, salaries, marketing, and other operating expenses — giving a fuller picture of profitability.

What's a 'good' profit margin?

It varies enormously by industry — grocery retail often runs on margins under 5%, while software businesses can see 70%+ gross margins. Compare against others in your specific industry rather than a universal benchmark.

How can I improve my margin?

Broadly: raise prices, reduce the cost of goods sold, or cut operating expenses — each has trade-offs specific to your business that this calculator can't account for.
