What this calculator does

Estimate a simple capital gain from purchase and sale values after eligible expenses, using a tax rate you enter. It keeps the assumptions visible so you can change one input at a time and understand what drives the result.

How the calculation works

Capital gain = sale proceeds − purchase cost − eligible expenses.

Inputs

The calculator uses Purchase price / cost basis, Sale proceeds, Purchase-related eligible costs, Selling costs, Assumed tax rate. Change the assumptions and click Calculate result to compare scenarios.

How to use the result responsibly

Do not treat one output as a prediction. Test conservative and optimistic assumptions, especially for rates, returns, inflation, taxes and property values. For loans and purchases, compare total cost rather than judging an offer only by monthly payment.

Limitations and methodology

Wealth Arrays performs deterministic browser-side calculations. It does not automatically know current market rates, lender policy, tax law, fees or personal eligibility. Verify current figures with the relevant official source or provider before making a material financial decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official tax calculator?

No. Actual treatment depends on asset, holding period, exemptions and local tax rules.

Can I subtract every expense?

Only costs allowed by the applicable tax rules should be deducted.

Does it handle indexation?

No. Use current official rules separately if indexation applies.
