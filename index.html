WHY WEALTH ARRAYS

Financial numbers are easier to use when the assumptions are visible

A financial result can look precise while hiding the assumptions that created it. Wealth Arrays combines calculators with plain-language explanations so you can see what a number means, change the inputs and compare scenarios. The goal is not to predict the future with a single number; it is to make the arithmetic behind a financial question easier to understand.

Our calculators cover recurring investments, compound growth, loans, returns, income and other everyday financial questions. Each tool is designed around a defined calculation model. Where timing, inflation, fees or other assumptions can materially change the result, the relevant guide or explanation points that out.

Use a calculator as a starting point

Begin with the question you actually need to answer. Enter a realistic baseline, then change one assumption at a time. For long-term planning, compare more than one scenario instead of treating an assumed return or inflation rate as a promise. For loans and taxes, compare the estimate with the current contract or official rules because generic calculators cannot know every product-specific condition.

We also publish original money guides covering the formulas behind the tools. If you want to understand a result before acting on it, read the relevant guide and methodology rather than relying only on the headline number.
