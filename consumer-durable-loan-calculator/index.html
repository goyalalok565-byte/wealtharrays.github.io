What this calculator does

Estimate EMI, interest and all-in cost for a phone, appliance or other financed purchase. It keeps the assumptions visible so you can change one input at a time and understand what drives the result.

How the calculation works

Financed amount = purchase price − down payment; EMI then follows standard amortizing-loan mathematics.

Inputs

The calculator uses purchase price, down payment, interest rate, term and processing fee. Change the assumptions and click Calculate result to compare scenarios.

How to use the result responsibly

Do not treat one output as a prediction. Test conservative and optimistic assumptions, especially for rates, returns, inflation, taxes and property values. Compare total cost rather than judging an offer only by the monthly payment.

Limitations and methodology

Wealth Arrays performs deterministic browser-side calculations. It does not automatically know current market rates, lender policy, tax law, fees or personal eligibility. Verify current figures with the relevant official source or provider before making a material decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is zero-interest financing really free?

Not necessarily; fees, bundled products or lost discounts can change the effective cost.

Why include processing fee?

Short loans can have a meaningful all-in cost from one-time fees.

Does this include late charges?

No.
