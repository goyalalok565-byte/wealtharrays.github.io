What this calculator does

Estimate the monthly payment and long-run cost of a home loan using a fixed interest rate and repayment term. It keeps the assumptions visible so you can change one input at a time and understand what drives the result.

How the calculation works

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1].

Inputs

The calculator uses Home loan amount, Annual interest rate, Loan term. Change the assumptions and click Calculate result to compare scenarios.

How to use the result responsibly

Do not treat one output as a prediction. Test conservative and optimistic assumptions, especially for rates, returns, inflation, taxes and property values. For loans and purchases, compare total cost rather than judging an offer only by monthly payment.

Limitations and methodology

Wealth Arrays performs deterministic browser-side calculations. It does not automatically know current market rates, lender policy, tax law, fees or personal eligibility. Verify current figures with the relevant official source or provider before making a material financial decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include property taxes and insurance?

No. It models the loan itself; recurring housing costs should be considered separately.

Why is total interest high on a long loan?

A longer term leaves the balance outstanding for more months, so interest accumulates for longer.

Can I use a variable-rate loan?

The model assumes the entered rate stays constant; recalculate when the applicable rate changes.
