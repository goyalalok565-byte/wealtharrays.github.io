# Overtime Pay Calculator

> Content draft only. Not wired into any calculator page or production route. Last reviewed: 2026-11-21. All examples are illustrative arithmetic, not product quotes or personalized advice.

## Overview

This calculator estimates gross pay from regular hours and overtime hours using a user-entered hourly rate and multiplier. It is a mathematical estimate, not a legal determination of overtime eligibility or the correct rate. Employment laws, exemptions, workweek definitions, collective agreements and employer policies differ by jurisdiction. Check the applicable labour authority and pay statement before treating an estimate as wages owed.

## How the estimate works

Regular pay = hourly rate × regular hours. Overtime pay = hourly rate × overtime hours × overtime multiplier. Estimated gross pay = regular pay + overtime pay. This simplified model assumes a single hourly rate and excludes bonuses, shift differentials and deductions.

## Worked examples

- At ₹500/hour, 40 regular hours and 8 overtime hours at 1.5×: regular ₹20,000; overtime ₹6,000; gross ₹26,000.
- At $30/hour, 40 regular hours and 5 overtime hours at 1.5×: regular $1,200; overtime $225; gross $1,425.

## Frequently asked questions

- **Is 1.5× universal?** No. It is a common rule in some jurisdictions for qualifying work, not a global rule.
- **Does working on a weekend always mean overtime?** Not necessarily; the legal workweek and applicable rules matter.
- **Does this include bonuses?** No. Some legal methods include specified remuneration when calculating a regular rate.
- **Is gross pay the take-home amount?** No. Taxes and deductions are not included.
- **Can the calculator decide if I am exempt?** No. Coverage and exemption are legal questions.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not assume overtime starts after a daily threshold or a weekend shift unless the applicable law says so. Do not treat the multiplier as universal. For US users, IRS qualified-overtime tax guidance is separate from the wage entitlement calculation; DOL overtime-pay page was not independently retrievable during this review and is therefore not linked.
