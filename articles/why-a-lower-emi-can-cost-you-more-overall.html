LOAN GUIDE

Why a Lower EMI Can Cost You More Overall

A smaller monthly loan payment can improve cash flow while increasing the total interest you pay.

Lower monthly payment does not mean cheaper loan

When the loan term becomes longer, the balance remains outstanding for more months. Interest therefore has more time to accumulate.

Compare two numbers every time

Look at the monthly payment and the total repayment together. A decision based only on EMI hides the full cost of borrowing.

Try the same loan with different terms

Keep the loan amount and interest rate fixed. Calculate a shorter term and a longer term. The shorter option will usually require a larger monthly payment but may substantially reduce total interest.

Choose based on cash flow, not illusion

The cheapest total loan is not automatically the best choice if the payment creates financial stress. The useful question is whether the higher payment is comfortably sustainable.

Bottom line

Compare affordability and total cost together. EMI answers one question; total repayment answers another.
