What this calculator does

Estimate a National Pension System corpus from a monthly contribution, an assumed annual return and a retirement horizon. Adjust the annuity allocation and annuity return to explore an illustrative retirement-income scenario. Change the assumptions to compare outcomes. The result is an estimate based on the inputs you provide, not a promise or personalised financial recommendation.

How the calculation works

For end-of-month contributions, estimated corpus = monthly contribution × (((1 + monthly rate)^number of months − 1) / monthly rate). The monthly rate is the assumed annual return divided by 12. If the rate is zero, corpus equals total contributions. Annuity allocation and monthly income are scenario estimates, not a guarantee or a statement of current NPS withdrawal rules.

How to use this calculator

For this calculator, check these inputs and units: Monthly NPS contribution; Assumed annual return; Years until retirement; Corpus allocated to annuity; Assumed annual annuity rate; Total contributions; Estimated investment growth; Estimated retirement corpus; Illustrative lump-sum allocation; Illustrative annuity allocation; Illustrative monthly annuity income before tax. Keep monthly and annual figures separate, and change one input at a time when comparing scenarios.

Read the result in context

The output is the mathematical result of the assumptions entered, not a forecast of what a bank, employer, tax authority or investment provider will necessarily apply. Separate amounts you contribute or pay from interest, growth, tax or other calculated components where the tool provides them. A larger final amount is not always a better outcome: total interest, liquidity, affordability, risk and the time available can matter just as much as the headline number. Keep a note of your inputs when comparing two scenarios.

Compare more than one scenario

Run a baseline estimate first. Then create a conservative case and a second case with one important assumption changed, such as the rate, period, payment or contribution. Comparing scenarios is more informative than selecting a single result because it shows how sensitive the outcome is to the inputs. For long periods, even a small change in an assumed rate can have a large effect. For short periods, fees, timing and fixed charges can be relatively more important than a percentage-rate difference.

What this estimate does not include

This calculator does not automatically know current product terms, policy changes, eligibility conditions, fees, taxes, penalties, contribution ceilings or personal circumstances. Those items can change the real amount you receive or pay. For tax-related estimates, verify the applicable tax year, regime and official rules. For deposits, loans and retirement products, compare the result with the provider current documentation. If the result will affect a major financial decision, confirm the assumptions with a qualified professional or the relevant official source.

Example inputs

One illustrative set of inputs is: Monthly NPS contribution: 5000; Assumed annual return: 9; Years until retirement: 25; Corpus allocated to annuity: 40; Assumed annual annuity rate: 6. Use your own figures for a more relevant estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Does this predict my actual NPS return?

No. NPS returns depend on the investment choices and market performance. Entered returns are assumptions, not guaranteed rates.

Does this calculator apply current NPS exit and tax rules?

It models the annuity allocation you enter and does not determine legal eligibility, withdrawal limits or tax treatment. Check current PFRDA and tax guidance before making a decision.

How is the monthly annuity estimate calculated?

It divides the estimated annuity allocation by 12 and multiplies it by the assumed annual annuity rate. Actual annuity products, rates, payout options and taxes may differ.

Important assumptions and limitations

This tool models the formula and assumptions described above. Actual outcomes can differ because of fees, taxes, eligibility rules, contribution timing, rate changes and individual circumstances. Check current official guidance or your provider's terms before making a financial decision.

For more context, read our calculator methodology and money guides. Results are educational estimates, not financial, tax, legal or investment advice.

NPS corpus and annuity are separate estimates

An NPS projection estimates how regular contributions could grow under an assumed return and retirement horizon. If the calculator also models an annuity allocation, it separates the amount allocated to an annuity from the remaining corpus. The return assumption is not guaranteed, and the annuity income estimate is not a quote from an insurer. Current exit, withdrawal, annuity and tax rules can change and are not fully determined by a simple projection.

Corpus is not the same as spendable income

The accumulated balance and the amount available as a lump sum may be subject to scheme exit rules and conditions. An annuity converts an allocated amount into a pension-like payment under the selected product terms; payout options, spouse benefits, insurer pricing and tax treatment affect the result. Do not interpret an assumed annuity rate as a guaranteed market quote.

Check official NPS rules before acting

Eligibility, withdrawal conditions, annuity allocation and tax treatment can depend on subscriber type, exit reason and current regulations. This calculator does not decide legal eligibility or process a withdrawal. Confirm the current rules with PFRDA and the official NPS service provider, and compare actual annuity illustrations before making a retirement decision.

Worked example with Indian rupees

At ₹5,000 per month for 25 years and a hypothetical 9% nominal annual return compounded monthly, total contributions are ₹15 lakh and the modelled corpus is about ₹56.06 lakh before fees and taxes. If 40% of that illustrative corpus were allocated to an annuity, the allocated amount would be about ₹22.42 lakh; at a hypothetical 6% annual annuity rate, the simple monthly income estimate is roughly ₹11,211 before tax and product-specific terms. These are scenarios, not a guarantee or insurer quote.
